About the conference

Connecting data, foresight and decision-making

Africa is experiencing profound and accelerating change — demographic growth, technological transformation, climate pressures, shifting geopolitics and evolving development pathways are reshaping the continent's future in complex, interconnected ways.

Convened by the African Futures and Innovation (AFI) programme at the Institute for Security Studies (ISS), the conference brings together policymakers, researchers, development practitioners, private-sector leaders and civil-society actors to explore how foresight can support better decisions for Africa's future.

Now in its third year, the conference connects research, foresight and policy engagement — with the practical application of long-term thinking at its centre.

Thematic tracks

Five lenses on Africa's future

Across all tracks, the conference promotes diverse voices and intergenerational dialogue on shaping the continent's future.

Africa's current path & development potential

An evidence-based look at where current trends are likely to lead — economic growth, employment, human development, governance, demographics and food systems.

Alternative futures

How Africa's future could unfold differently — the role of policy, leadership, innovation, investment and institutions in shaping alternative pathways.

Preferred futures for Africa

What inclusive, sustainable and desirable futures could look like, connecting closely with Agenda 2063 and continental development aspirations.

Building futures capacity

Hands-on futures lab sessions and workshops covering futures literacy, scenario development, horizon scanning and participatory foresight.

Foresight for decision-making & impact

Real-world examples of foresight informing policy — integrating foresight into national planning and institutionalising it within government.
